Why Google Maps

Ubiquity as a feature

Google Maps is what most users already know how to pan and search. I reach for it when product familiarity and Places data outweigh MapLibre-style open tiling.

User muscle memory

Store locators, trip planners, and consumer apps often convert better when the map already feels like Google.

Places & POIs

Rich place data and autocomplete save months of gazetteer work — when licensing fits the project.

Routes & mobility

Directions and distance matrix APIs pair cleanly with product backends and mobile clients.

Know the tradeoffs

Closed styles and pricing tiers push some GIS workloads elsewhere. This lab documents when Google is — and isn't — the right call.
